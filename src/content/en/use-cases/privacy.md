## What this site doesn't learn

- **Not the content.** Your secret is encrypted in your browser and the key never reaches the server.
- **Not who you are.** There are no accounts, and we keep no per-secret access logs or IP history.
- **Not when you made it.** The stored expiry time is rounded, so it can't be turned back into a creation time.
- **Not your visit to anyone else.** Every font, script and image comes from this site; no third party is contacted, and there are no cookies or analytics.

## Works where you browse

shhnote.com works in Tor Browser at the "Safer" security level. At "Safest", JavaScript is off, so pages still read normally but creating or opening a secret needs a lower level, and the page says so.

## Worth knowing

The [security page](/en/security/) lists the honest caveats too: anyone with the full link can open a one-time secret, so treat the link as the secret, and close the tab when you're done reading.
