## The problem

Initial credentials sent by email or posted in a ticket outlive their purpose. They sit in mailboxes and ticket histories long after the person has changed their password.

## How shhnote.com helps

- **One link per person.** Create a separate one-time secret for each new starter, so each link is read once, by one person.
- **Files and text together.** Attach a VPN profile or a certificate alongside the instructions.
- **Proof of a single read.** If the recipient finds the link already opened, something else read it first, and you know to reset the credential.
- **A short expiry.** Pick a lifetime that covers the first day, so unopened links clean themselves up.
- **Automation.** For many starters at once, the documented [API](/en/docs/) creates links from your own scripts.

## Good habits

Send the link and any passphrase through different channels, for example the link by email and the passphrase in person or by phone.
