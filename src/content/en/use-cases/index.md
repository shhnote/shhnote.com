- [DevOps & Engineering: Share credentials and keys, not chat history](/en/use-cases/developers/). API keys, database passwords and private keys end up in chat threads and tickets, where they stay searchable for years. A one-time link keeps them out.
- [Privacy-conscious users: Share something without leaving a trail](/en/use-cases/privacy/). Some things should be shared without anyone, including the site in the middle, learning more than they must.
- [IT & Onboarding: Hand out first-day credentials safely](/en/use-cases/onboarding/). A new starter needs a password, a VPN profile and the Wi-Fi key. None of them should sit in an email thread afterwards.
- [Legal & Regulatory: Send sensitive records without leaving copies](/en/use-cases/legal/). A record sent as an email attachment stays in two mailboxes and every backup of them. A link that is deleted after reading doesn't.
- [Personal: Everyday secrets, simply](/en/use-cases/personal/). Not every secret is a production database key. Most are a Wi-Fi password, a door code or a document you'd rather not leave in a chat.
- [Misc: Gift codes and surprise reveals](/en/use-cases/gifts/). A one-time link is a nice way to deliver something that should be seen once: a gift code, a surprise, the next clue.
