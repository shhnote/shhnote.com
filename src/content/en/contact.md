## General questions

For questions, feedback or anything else, email us at [contact@shhnote.com](mailto:contact@shhnote.com).

## Reporting a security issue

If you've found a vulnerability, please tell us privately first so we can fix it before it's made public: [security@shhnote.com](mailto:security@shhnote.com). Our [security page](/en/security/#reporting-a-vulnerability) says what to include and what to expect from us.

## Reporting a link

If you hold a secret link that shares something it shouldn't, use the [report page](/en/report/). If the secret is still active it is removed immediately.
